This is the same playground served by `npm run demo`, using the local `@bloxwap/chart` build. Open it in fullscreen or a separate tab for more room.

## Try the controls

1. Drag the canvas to pan and scroll to zoom.
2. Use the **15m** button in the left control rail to switch timeframes.
3. Open **Chart type** or **Indicators** to try all five series types and seven built-in indicators.
4. Choose a drawing tool in the rail and place its points on the chart.
5. Use **Undo** and **Redo** to experiment with your drawings.
6. Open the bottom-left gear to change candles, scales, labels, canvas appearance, and sample datasets.
7. Switch the chart between light and dark, or collapse the drawing rail for more canvas space.

On small screens, the drawing rail starts collapsed. Use the bottom-left toolbar toggle to show it.

Prices are simulated and update every second. Pause them with **Live feed** in the settings card's **Canvas** tab. Volume and example drawings are included to get you started.

Click inside the chart to use its keyboard shortcuts, including undo/redo. The embedded playground keeps those shortcuts separate from documentation search and page navigation.
